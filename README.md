## Projects

Native macOS apps, all free and open source. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no
accounts. I build them because the app I wanted either didn't exist or sat
behind a payment.

### 🌙 [Muro](https://github.com/MrRockySL/Muro) &nbsp;·&nbsp; Swift

Live video wallpapers for your Mac, running at around 2% CPU.

Most live wallpaper apps are Electron wrapping a web page. They idle at 300 to
400 MB of RAM and keep the fan audible, for a picture that moves. Muro is
native Swift and SwiftUI, hands decoding to the Apple Silicon media engine, and
pauses itself the moment you can't see it, which costs nothing at all. Browse a
full screen gallery, apply to every display, build playlists, or drop in your
own videos.

New wallpapers arrive on their own from a hosted catalog, so the library keeps
growing without anyone having to update the app.

### 🔊 [Unduck Pro](https://github.com/MrRockySL/Unduck-Pro) &nbsp;·&nbsp; Swift

Stops macOS quietly dropping your music to a whisper the moment a call starts.

macOS ducks other audio during FaceTime, Zoom and Meet, and gives you no switch
to turn it off. Unduck Pro is a menu bar app that keeps media at full volume,
and throws in a per app volume mixer and output switcher while it's there. I
built it because every other fix I could find was a paid app.

### 🏝️ Islet &nbsp;·&nbsp; Swift &nbsp;·&nbsp; *not public yet*

A Dynamic Island for the Mac, living in the notch.

The notch is dead space. Islet turns it into the most useful strip on the
screen. Hover and it opens into a glass panel holding media controls for any
player, your calendar, a shelf that shuffles files around faster than Finder,
AirDrop, and a converter that handles images, PDFs, video and audio without
uploading anything anywhere. It also shows live Claude Code and Antigravity
usage limits, so you know what's left before starting a session, and glows when
an agent finishes and wants you back.

On Macs and monitors without a notch it shows up as a floating pill instead, so
it follows you across every screen. Idles near zero CPU at about 55 MB.

Still polishing this one. It'll be here when it's ready.
